description: >-
  This cabinet-refinishing project transforms the original natural-oak raised-panel cabinetry with
  a Perfect Khaki perimeter finish and a deeper Teakwood finish on the island, paired with brushed
  nickel bar pulls. Bright white solid-surface countertops extend across the U-shaped work area and
  oversized central island, while a gray-and-taupe linear mosaic backsplash adds texture. A
  stainless undermount sink sits beneath the window, complemented by white appliances and geometric
  dark-metal pendants. Light wood flooring and matching laundry-room upper cabinets create a warm,
  cohesive transitional design.
