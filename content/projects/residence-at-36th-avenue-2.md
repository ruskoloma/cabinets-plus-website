  This open kitchen features crisp white shaker cabinetry with warm brass pulls, complemented by a
  light natural-wood island base and pale wood flooring. A large waterfall island in white

  continue beneath a wide window and farmhouse sink. Stainless steel appliances and stacked wall
  ovens add a contemporary edge. White-and-gold dome pendants, a wood ceiling beam, and an adjacent
  stone fireplace create a warm, airy transitional feel.
